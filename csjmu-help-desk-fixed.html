Maintenance Desk

Report a campus issue

AI routes it to the right team instantly

AI also reads your description and may raise the priority if it spots a safety risk.
Start describing the problem — AI will pick the right team and priority.

How it works

  1. Describe the issue and where it is.
  2. AI reads it and assigns the correct team (electrical, plumbing, civil, IT, housekeeping, grounds, safety or lifts).
  3. It sets a priority and a target resolution time.
  4. You get a MNT- reference number to track progress.
In an emergency (fire, electric shock, injury) call 112 or campus security first, then report here.
